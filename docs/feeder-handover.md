The camera spots birds at your feeder and sends their photos to the app. It
needs power and your Wi-Fi. Nothing else at your house needs setting up.

## Put it up

1. Pick a window with no insect screen. The mesh blurs every photo.
2. Put the feeder or a seed dish close to the window. About 1 metre from the
   glass is best. Further away, the birds look too small to name.
3. Press the camera lens flat against the glass. Hold the board with tape or
   Blu Tack on its edges, never on the lens.
4. Turn the board so its ribbon cable leaves from the bottom. Upside down, every
   photo comes out upside down.
5. Hang a dark cloth behind the camera. It stops the room and lamps reflecting
   in the glass.
6. Plug it in, and wait two minutes.

## Aim it with the Live tab

1. Open the app on your phone. Tailscale must be on.
2. Go to **Live**. You see what the camera sees.
3. Move the camera until the feeder sits in the middle of the picture.
4. Check the picture at night with the room lights on. If you see the room in
   the glass, fix the cloth.
5. Call me once it is in place. I set the part of the picture it watches.

## What the Feeders tab tells you

| The card says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Online | All is well. | Nothing. |
| Low signal | It works, but the Wi-Fi is weak or it missed a check-in. | Move the router or the feeder closer if it lasts a day. |
| Offline | Nothing heard for 30 minutes. | Check the plug. Then see "If your Wi-Fi changes". |
| Feed may be low, or Feed may be out | Birds stopped coming. | Refill it, then tap **Refilled**. |
| A number waiting to upload | The photos are safe on the camera. My computer is off. | Nothing. They upload when it comes back. |
| GPU not answering, or Running hot | Something on my side. | Call me if it lasts a day. |

A power cut does no harm. The camera starts again on its own.

## If your Wi-Fi changes

A new router, a new Wi-Fi name, or a new password cuts the camera off.

**Easiest:** give the new router the old Wi-Fi name and the old password. The
camera then joins it by itself.

**Otherwise:**

1. Turn on your phone's hotspot. Its name and password must be the ones it had
   when I set up the camera. The name is at the top of this page. On an iPhone,
   the hotspot name is the phone's name, in Settings, General, About.
2. Put the phone near the feeder. On an iPhone, keep the Personal Hotspot screen
   open, and turn on **Maximize Compatibility**.
3. Call me. Within a few minutes the camera joins your phone, and I can reach it.
4. I enter the new Wi-Fi from my end. When I say so, turn the hotspot off.

The camera uses a little of your phone data while it is on the hotspot.

## Leave alone

- The memory card in the small board, and the ribbon cable. Both are fragile.
- The power supply. Use the one it came with.
